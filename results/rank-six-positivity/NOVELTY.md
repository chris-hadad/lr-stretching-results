# Related work and the scope of the contribution

The main claim is an all-size rank-six coefficient theorem. Its distinctive
proof step is a rigorously justified restriction of a complete correction
system to supports that can carry positive actual hive-face weight. The
unrestricted balanced identity remains intact. The package also supplies
original-lattice scalar and support algorithms, exact finite fields, and
independent verification.

The following distinctions prevent an inflated novelty claim.

- [Ferudun's rank-five theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22301v2), version 2
  dated 5 September 2026, precedes this result. Its normal correction and
  polygon-closure method are important antecedents. The current work extends
  the rank scope and supplies a realizability restriction and complete
  original-fan treatment; it does not originate the idea of balanced correction.
- [Berline–Vergne's local formula](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0507256v3), and
  its dual solid valuation, are classical analytic premises. Refinement,
  quotient metric and normalized lattice measures must use those statements
  at their exact scopes.
- The scalar algorithms combine classical finite lattice numerators,
  reciprocity, character orthogonality and
  [holomorphic projection](https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.00426v2). The explicit
  recurrences and their hive-specific computational use are explained in the
  manuscript; no general invention of those classical methods is claimed.
- [Ghodsi's finite-cover preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14357v1), submitted
  13 September 2026, concerns seven parts with outer size at most thirty.
  That is a different bounded statement. Our earlier finite-box result is
  preserved separately; neither finite-box result proves the all-size rank-six
  theorem.
- The small q1–q3 replay provides a compact rank-six verification of the top
  coefficients. It does not claim the first rank-uniform positivity theorem
  for those coefficients.
- The [legal-realization converse and complete closure calculus](GEOMETRY.md)
  are additional geometric results. The all-rank converse preserves legal
  integral boundaries and original saturated face weights. At rank six, the
  complete 103-identity presentation and the minimum-six obstruction are
  certified statements about every physical row set. Positive-identity
  propagation, the closure sandwich and RUP proof checking use standard
  convex-geometric and logical principles. No claim is made that the displayed
  six-versus-six algebraic identity itself is new to the literature.

This is a bounded primary-source comparison checked on 25 September 2026.
Failure to find an equivalent result does not establish exhaustive priority.
The finite certificate and internal reviews are also separate from external
human mathematical acceptance.
