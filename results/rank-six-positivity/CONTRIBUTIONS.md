## Model and harness evidence

The preserved native seat record identifies the Opus consultation, route
synthesis and E2 exploration as `claude-opus-5-5[1m]`, and the native audit as
`claude-fable-5-1`. The recorded Codex consultation has a Sonnet transport
wrapper: its wrapper metadata says `claude-sonnet-5`, while its preserved
mathematical response identifies `gpt-6-astra`. That supports a qualified
backend attribution, not a claim that a lower-level execution trace was
independently checked. The route ledger assigns O1 to E2.

Four additional verification seats were dispatched on the `opus` alias. Their
roles should be distinguished:

- One fresh seat examined the pruning lemma against the complete normal-cycle
  argument. Its verdict retained the underlying geometric and finite premises.
- A report explicitly naming Opus 5.5 independently classified 44,979 distinct
  rows using exact linear-programming certificates. This was a sampled check.
- A second report explicitly naming Opus 5.5 supplied a separate full-file
  implementation: its class and closure files agreed on all 27,230,728 rows,
  and it independently recounted the q9 domain. Implementation agreement was
  reported as computation, separately from the mathematical support lemma.
- The whole-count seat checked the identity on 13 triples. Four campaign-seed
  linear coefficients initially came from an earlier exploration record;
  only two subsequently received a second-engine confirmation. None of those
  subjects exercised an excluded support. This was a useful bounded control,
  not an independent proof of every numerical premise.

The first and fourth seats have observed `opus` dispatch evidence but no
provider-resolved version in the inspected records. The public account does
not silently assign them the explicit 5.5 identifier of the other two reports.
Other Opus 5.5 explorations reported positive deformation-cone examples,
local Euler--Maclaurin identities and exact obstructions to proposed cluster,
orientation and concavity routes. Those reported findings informed the investigation;
their bounded scopes do not become additional proofs of the whole theorem.

Source maps preserve the exact selected source identities. Public materials
contain curated mathematics and necessary data/checkers, rather than raw
private conversations or orchestration records. The [AI-assistance account](../../AI-ASSISTANCE.md)
explains how the different research environments built on and challenged prior
work. No controlled model-comparison experiment was performed.
