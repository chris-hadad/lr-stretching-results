# Source comparison and significance

Primary sources checked on 14 September 2026:

- Igor Pak and Ernesto Vallejo, [Combinatorics and geometry of Littlewood-Richardson cones](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~pak/papers/liri91.pdf), Section 3 and Lemma 3.1. The row-letter inequalities and their tableau interpretation are classical. The explicit seven-coordinate specialization is used here with its full integer inverse; it is not presented as a new LR rule.
- Etienne Rassart, [A polynomiality property for Littlewood-Richardson coefficients](https://arxiv.org/html/math/0308101v2), Section 4 and Corollary 4.2. Polynomiality on the relevant parameter cones supplies period collapse. Polynomiality alone is not positivity.
- Nicole Berline and Michele Vergne, [Local Euler-Maclaurin formula for polytopes](https://arxiv.org/html/math/0507256v3), the local formula and the dual-solid subdivision property in Corollary 23. These supply the analytic valuation behind the finite constants; numerical agreement does not replace that theorem.
- Alper Ferudun, [KTT positivity reproducibility bundle](https://github.com/AlperTheKing/ktt-positivity), current README and general-status record. The live primary README states the version-two all-size theorem through rank five. Earlier cached search descriptions still saying four parts were not used as the current frontier.
- Ronald King, Christophe Tollu and Frédéric Toumazet, [The hive model and the polynomial nature of stretched Littlewood-Richardson coefficients](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~slc/s/s54Akitoto1.pdf), for stretching questions, factorization and degree examples. A bounded or structural degree statement is not a positivity proof at every boundary.

The new certificate covers an entire rank-six family at every area and
parameter wall, including degree-seven objects. Its statement is broader
within rank six than the earlier transportation and four-capacity
constructors. The rank-seven 3-by-5 transportation theorem retains its own
scope and is not absorbed into a rank-six result.

The complete finite fields and original-lattice verification provide a
useful standalone advance. The explicit interior translation and grouped
successive-overlap formula provide additional exact count methods. The
limited source comparison did not identify the same complete six-row,
three-letter positivity theorem. That is not an exhaustive literature search,
priority determination, or claim that every intermediate construction is new.

The full proof, exact data, source maps and reproducible programs are suitable
for independent mathematical scrutiny and incremental sharing. A research
note is justified by the complete family result. Expert assessment is still
needed to judge novelty and a possible paper; no collaboration or authorship
commitment is inferred. Whole ordinary-rank-six positivity and unrestricted
KTT remain separate open goals.
