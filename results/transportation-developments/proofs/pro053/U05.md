## 3. Full coefficient kernels, including their signed small indices

The elementary rising-addition identities, including both endpoints, are


```text
a_j(X+Y)=sum_(i=0)^j d_i(Y)a_(j-i)(X),
```

```text

```

This is the full convolution sum_(I=0)^x a_(n-1)(I)d_k(2x-I). The terminal factor is a_n, not d_n.

For k>=2 set


```text

                     +(k-2)binom(x+n+k-3,n+k),
D2_(n,1)=2d_(n+1)-a_n,   D2_(n,0)=a_n.
```

The all-index derivation retains the double-boundary multiplicity s+1. For k>=2,


```text
sum_(s>=0)(s+1)a_k(s-2)w^s
    =w^2[3+(k-2)w]/(1-w)^(k+2).
```

At k=1 this generating function is (3w-1)/(1-w)^3, and at k=0 it is 1/(1-w)^2. Convolution with a_(n-2)(x-s) proves every D2 formula, including the signed exceptional endpoint. Put



Direct simplification yields


