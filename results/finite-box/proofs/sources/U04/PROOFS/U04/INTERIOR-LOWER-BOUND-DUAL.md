# A four-count sign dual retaining an uncomputed nonnegative third interior

Let P(t)=sum_(k=0)^5 c_k t^k be an entire nonempty period-one LR polynomial with actual dimension five. Assume its complete saturated chart has primitive inward normals of squared norm at most six. Thus c3>0 by the adopted complete two-cone theorem and c4,c5>0 intrinsically. Set A=P(1), B=P(2), U=I(1), V=I(2), W=I(3), where I is the true relative interior count. All five interior/closed objects use the same whole polytope and saturated lattice; the counting representation may be changed only by a proved full map.

The following are exact identities on the full prior degree-at-most-five polynomial space, using P(0)=1 and reciprocity P(-j)=-I(j):

    60*c1 = 30*A - 3*B + 60*U - 15*V + 2*W + 20,
    24*c2 = 16*(A-U) - (B-V) - 30.

To verify the first without any fit, for each monomial t^k, k=0,...,5, the functional

    30*P(1)-3*P(2)-60*P(-1)+15*P(-2)-2*P(-3)+20*P(0)

has value 60 at k=1 and zero at every other k. Substitution of reciprocity gives the identity. The second follows by the even-part cancellation, as in the inherited quintic decision proof.

Since W is the cardinality of a complete finite set, W>=0 without evaluating it. Therefore the four actually counted values A,B,U,V imply

    c1 >= L1/60,
    L1 = 30*A-3*B+60*U-15*V+20.

Consequently L1>=0 and D2=16*(A-U)-(B-V)-30>=0 certify every ordinary coefficient. This is a genuine incomplete-count rational sign certificate: the nonnegative unknown W is retained in the proof, not asserted zero, inferred from a panel, or interpolated away. No complete coefficient vector is reconstructed by these four counts. It handles nonempty I2 as written. It does not claim every actual quintic satisfies L1>=0.

The criterion complements the inherited D1 certificate: D1=12*c1-48*c5, so D1>=0 also proves c1>=0 because c5>0. The union of the two certified halfspaces can only improve coverage. A negative D1 or L1 alone is not a negative ordinary coefficient. A negative D2 is a genuine c2 observation with these premises at the stated scope. Outside this exact chart/dimension domain, neither the top-coefficient protection nor the parity is silently carried over.
