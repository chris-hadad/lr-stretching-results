# Prior work and novelty assessment

Assessment date: 14 September 2026.

[Alper Ferudun's version-two preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22301v2)
proves positivity through length five and supplies rank-uniform top-four
coefficient protection for full-dimensional hives. The accompanying
[primary repository](https://github.com/AlperTheKing/ktt-positivity) was checked
at c3a0795bd287dcca78fac2cc6ba4282144bb7813. Its correction methods and the
Berline-Vergne local formula are foundational sources for the present work.

Earlier results in this collection protect ambient rank-six c6 through c10 and
several complete families and boundary-coordinate classes. The c4/c5 fields
extend the protected range to every rank-six area and boundary. The
actual-degree consequences combine the existing strict-witness/Horn argument
with later coordinate theorems; their prior ingredients retain full credit.

Targeted searches and comparison with the current primary sources found no
earlier theorem matching the new universal c4/c5 scope. This supports treating
the result as a substantive extension of the inspected prior work. The search
is bounded and does not establish worldwide priority. The repository records
an independently checked computational contribution for further scrutiny;
human peer review and publication suitability remain separate assessments.

The broader rank-six and unrestricted KTT problems remain open. This result
is shared at its standalone partial scope, with the complete proof, finite
certificates and reproducible verifier linked from the result page.
