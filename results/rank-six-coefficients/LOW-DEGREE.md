# Actual-degree consequences through ordinary rank seven

Every stretched LR polynomial of ordinary rank at most six and actual degree
at most five is coefficientwise nonnegative. The same holds at ordinary rank
at most seven and actual degree at most four. These statements include all
areas and boundaries, with lambda outer and the original saturated hive lattice.

This consequence combines the earlier
[strict-witness and Horn-face argument](../families-and-obstructions/proofs/actual-cubics.md)
with the all-size [four-coordinate theorem](../structural-positivity/proofs/pro027/023-ALL-SIZE-FOUR-COORDINATE-LR-POSITIVITY.md)
and [rank-six five-coordinate theorem](../structural-positivity/proofs/pro027/028-ALL-RANK6-FIVE-COORDINATE-POSITIVITY.md).
Their complete source certificates and original verification scopes remain
the premises. No new replay of the historical witness arrays is claimed here.

## Rank six

Ranks at most five are covered by Ferudun's theorem. Empty positive-stretch
families have polynomial zero. Consider a nonempty final-rank-six family.

On a proper multiplicity-one essential Horn facet, the entire polynomial
factors into selected and complementary child polynomials, both of rank at
most five. Each factor is nonnegative, so their product is nonnegative.

Away from proper Horn facets, use an allowed whole-count symmetry image and
its sound coordinate-identification closure. If some image has at most five
selected coordinates, the all-size five-coordinate theorem applies.
Otherwise every image has at least six coordinates. The complete strict-witness
transfer proves that the closure chart attains its predicted affine dimension
on that Horn-strict relative boundary face. The actual degree is therefore
at least six, contrary to the hypothesis. This exhausts the rank-six cases.

The argument uses the Horn-strict branch explicitly. A large coordinate upper
bound alone never establishes the actual degree.

## Rank seven

For final rank seven on a proper Horn facet, a 2+5 or 3+4 split has two
Ferudun-protected children. A 1+6 split has a nonempty constant rank-one factor;
the rank-six child's degree is at most the parent degree and the preceding
theorem applies. Empty products give zero.

On the Horn-strict branch, an image with at most four selected coordinates is
covered by the four-coordinate theorem. If every image has at least five,
the same complete strict-witness transfer forces actual degree at least five,
again a contradiction. Hence every rank-seven polynomial of actual degree at
most four is nonnegative.

Together with the new universal c4/c5 bounds and earlier higher-index
protection, the remaining rank-six question is c1,c2,c3 at actual degrees six
through ten, outside all existing whole-positive terminals. This identifies a
logical residual, not a finite or minimal feasible census. Horn-strict rank-seven
quintics remain a separate open class.
