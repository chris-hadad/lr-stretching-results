# Review of the researcher edition

An independent AI review examined the mathematical presentation, verification
claims, source attribution and reproduction instructions for the September 13
edition at commit `64e18347b1bbd7cb3e881d61b369aaa27d5dc602`.
It identified one provenance-labeling error: the first draft of a new guide
was attributed to an older commit that did not contain it. The source record
now distinguishes that unpublished draft from the files in the earlier Git
snapshot. The correction changed no mathematical statement, program or data.

The [execution record](PUBLICATION-CHECKS.md) describes the computations
actually performed. The edition review is separate from those executions,
the earlier mathematical proof checks and external human peer review.
Contributions and the independence of the different checks are explained in
[PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md).
