# Mathematical contract for the rank-six certificates

Write

    P(t) = c^(t lambda)_(t mu,t nu) = sum_j c_j t^j,

with lambda outer and balanced nonnegative integral partitions of maximum
trimmed length at most six. The claim is

    c_k >= (1/2000000) sum_(actual k-faces F) vol_Z(F), k=4,5.

The sum uses every actual face and its saturated affine lattice, with ordinary
lattice-normalized volume, rather than factorial-normalized volume. A nonempty
hive of actual dimension at least k has an actual k-face of positive volume,
so c_k>0. If its dimension is below k, the coefficient is zero. An empty
positive-stretch family has polynomial zero; the all-empty triple has polynomial
one. Ordinary rank, actual dimension and coefficient index have different roles.

This is a computer-assisted sufficient certificate under the standard LR/hive
correspondence, saturation, stretching polynomiality and degree/dimension
theorems, together with the Berline-Vergne local formula. Its finite predicates
are completely reproduced by `verify.py --full`. Mathematical interpretation
of those predicates is given below. No whole rank-six or unrestricted KTT
positivity claim follows for the remaining low coefficients.

## 1. Original normals and complete finite coverage

Pad to rank six. The conventional hive has interior coordinates

    (1,1),(1,2),(1,3),(1,4),(2,1),(2,2),(2,3),(3,1),(3,2),(4,1)

in the original lattice Z^10. All 45 inward rhombus inequalities yield 42
distinct oriented primitive normals. Repeated directions retain their original
rhombi; no facet is dropped because a sampled boundary made it redundant.

For k=5 use q=10-k=5 independent normals; for k=4 use q=6. The topology core
rebuilds the rhombi and the six barycentric coordinate permutations. These
actions are integral orthogonal maps of the ten-coordinate lattice. It checks
every original q-subset and dependent complement, every canonical orbit and
every type assignment. At index one, a generator-permuted Gram matrix identifies
the complete saturated normal lattice and metric. At higher index the checker
requires all ten signed-permuted ambient columns, including repeated and zero
columns. No equal-Gram/equal-index shortcut is used.

## 2. Complete local constants

Let N have q independent rows. For T contained in those rows define

    L_T=N_T Z^10,  I_T=[Z^T:L_T],  H_T=N_T N_T^T,  I_empty=1.

The normal-coordinate image lattice has induced metric H_T^(-1). Its
primitive positive axis length at i is r_(T,i)=I_T/I_(T without i). Coordinate
projection gives an exact lattice sequence, so the face-integral normalization
for a removed coordinate set U includes I_(T without U)/I_T.

The complete numerator consists of all p in L_T satisfying
0<=p_i<r_(T,i). Its size is product_i r_(T,i)/I_T. The program obtains the image
indices by unimodular integer column reduction and enumerates the complete
finite residue group generated by the original columns. Cardinality and every
primitive divisor are checked.

Choose one generic vector w and use c_T=H_T^(-1)w_T. These covectors are the
compatible orthogonal projections of one ambient specialization. Put

    S_T(s) = sum_p exp(s*c_T.p) / product_i(1-exp(s*r_(T,i)*c_(T,i))).

The local Euler-Maclaurin recurrence, with mu_empty=1, is

    mu_T(s) = S_T(s)
      - sum_(nonempty U subset T)
        [(-1)^|U| I_(T without U)/(I_T*s^|U|*product_(i in U)c_(T,i))]
        * mu_(T without U)(s).

The transverse constant is [s^0]mu_full(s). Every quotient and removed face
is included. Multiplication by s^|T| reduces the calculation to coefficients
through degree q; the child ranges align exactly after each integral factor.
The denominator expansion includes the degree-six Bernoulli term. An explicit
control shows that omitting it can preserve pole cancellation while changing
the answer. The verifier checks every stored value by a fresh exact rational
reconstruction, including all raw negatives.

The analytic recurrence and dual-solid valuation are from
[Berline and Vergne, Local Euler-Maclaurin formula for polytopes](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0507256).
The complete lattice conversion, numerator and truncation argument specializes
that theorem; numerical agreement is not substituted for the analytic premise.

## 3. Primitive corrections and exact arithmetic

For every independent (q-1)-support J, a supplied integer matrix M_J has
columns spanning its saturated kernel. The checker verifies J*M_J=0 and an
integer left inverse L_J*M_J=I, with the full expected rank. For I=J union {n},
the primitive quotient conormal is M_J^T*n divided by the gcd of all its
coordinates. The divisor equals index(I)/index(J).

Assign rational linear functionals h_J and set

    beta_I = alpha_I + sum_(J facet of I) h_J(u_(I/J)).

Average over every element of the six-action stabilizer coset. Every equivariant
field is represented by the support-orbit coordinates, and averaging a full
field preserves its lower bound. Multiplication by six clears the averaging
denominator. The checker independently reconstructs every integer matrix entry,
all cancellations and every omitted zero.

The rational fields have common denominators 10^10 and 10^12. If alpha=a/b
and the field numerators are z_j, each row is checked entirely with integers:

    numerator   = 6*D*a + b*sum_j B_ij*z_j,
    denominator = 6*D*b,
    2000000*numerator >= denominator > 0.

Every row and every available column is retained, including rows whose raw
constant is negative. The exact minima are

    c5 field: 25239007/25245000000000, at row 291;
    c4 field: 3412401888539617/3407104701000000000000, at row 192.

These are verified margins, without a claim of optimality or minimum support.
The full field, rather than a collection of unchecked local patches, is used.

## 4. Passage to every whole hive

First clear all vertex denominators by a positive integer dilation. This gives
an integral polytope with the same normal directions. Both c_k and every
k-face volume scale by the same kth power. No polynomiality of a perturbed
polytope is required.

The full normal fan admits a compatible pointed simplicial refinement using
the same original normal rays, even when the hive has hidden affine equalities.
One construction loosens every original inequality by a generic positive small
amount. The resulting bounded polytope is full-dimensional and simple; every
vertex basis limits to an original vertex, so its fan refines the original
fan. Only that fan is used; no Ehrhart coefficient is transferred by continuity.

Give each q-cone of this refinement the normalized volume of the actual k-face
whose coarse normal cone contains its relative interior, or zero when there
is no such face. At every (q-1)-cone, the complete weighted sum of primitive
quotient conormals is zero. At an actual (k+1)-face this is lattice-normalized
facet balance. Within a subdivided coarse q-cone, the two adjacent primitive
directions cancel with equal weights. Inside a larger coarse cone every weight
is zero. These cases include lineality and all hidden strata.

The BV dual-solid valuation adds local constants over the full-dimensional
subdivision cells; its ambient/subspace compatibility retains the actual
transverse lattice. Thus

    c_k = sum_sigma w_sigma*alpha_sigma
        = sum_sigma w_sigma*beta_sigma.

The second equality follows by collecting each h_J against its entire balanced
quotient sum. Since all w_sigma>=0 and every beta_sigma>=1/2000000, and every
actual k-face contributes at least one refinement cell, the stated complete
face-volume bound follows. Dividing out the denominator-clearing dilation
returns the original period-one rational hive.

This is the complete refined-normal-cycle argument developed in the campaign's
Pro027 work, extending its earlier saturated facet-balance framework. Alper

the new Pro028 fields establish the displayed rank-six coefficients. A failed
