\subsection{Exact legal realization of retained branches}\label{sec:realization}
The support criterion has a constructive geometric converse, valid beyond
the rank-six certificate. Put \(m=(n-1)(n-2)/2\) and
\(T_n=\{(i,j)\in\Z_{\geq0}^2:i+j\leq n\}\), retaining every physical
rhombus on \(T_n\).

\begin{theorem}[Legal realization]\label{thm:realization}
Let \(n\geq3\), and let \(S\) contain \(q\) physical rows with independent
interior normals, where \(1\leq q\leq m\). Then \(\rank_x\cl(S)=q\) if
and only if there are a legal integral LR boundary and a compatible positive
generic original-row relaxation in which the selected physical branch \(S\)
defines a \(q\)-dimensional normal cell of positive actual
\((m-q)\)-face weight. The parent may be lower-dimensional or nonsimple.
The face uses the original saturated integer lattice.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Use the boundary convention of Section~\ref{sec:model}. For arbitrary \(n\),
the three types of rhombi, where all four points exist, are
\begin{align*}
A_{i,j}&=-h(i,j)+h(i+1,j)+h(i,j+1)-h(i+1,j+1),\\
B_{i,j}&=-h(i,j)+h(i,j+1)+h(i-1,j+1)-h(i-1,j+2),\\
C_{i,j}&=-h(i,j)+h(i-1,j+1)+h(i-1,j)-h(i-2,j+1).
\end{align*}
Define the three boundary words by successive differences along the same
horizontal, vertical and diagonal edges. Their trace is
\(|\lambda|=|\mu|+|\nu|\). Direct cancellation gives
\begin{align*}
\mu_i-\mu_{i+1}&=A_{i-1,0}+C_{i+1,0},\\
\lambda_j-\lambda_{j+1}&=A_{0,j-1}+B_{1,j-1},\\
\nu_j-\nu_{j+1}&=B_{n-j,j-1}+C_{n-j+1,j-1}.
\end{align*}
All words are therefore weakly decreasing. For integral heights set
\[
u=\max(0,-\mu_n),\qquad v=\max(0,-\lambda_n,u-\nu_n).
\]
Adding \(ui+vj-h(0,0)\) to \(h(i,j)\) changes the words to
\((\lambda+v,\mu+u,\nu+v-u)\), which are nonnegative integral partitions.
Every rhombus slack and the trace are unchanged. The fixed-boundary interior
lattice is translated by an integer vector.

Choose a rational relative-interior point of the universal face defined by
\(S\), clear its denominators by a positive integer scaling, and apply this
legalization. Write it as \((b,x_*)\), and set \(T=\cl(S)\).
Exactly the rows \(T\) are tight. In the fixed-boundary hive, the face cut
out by \(S\) has direction space \(\ker_{\R}N_T\): every \(T\)-row
vanishes on it, while sufficiently small displacements in either direction
of that kernel preserve every other strict inequality. Its dimension is
\(m-q\), with affine lattice \(x_*+\ker_{\Z}N_T\). This kernel is
saturated, directly from the integer matrix equations.

The hive is bounded. A recession direction \(d\) has zero boundary.
Summing \(A_{a,b}(d)\) over \(0\leq a<i,\ 0\leq b<j\) gives
\(-d(i,j)\), hence \(d(i,j)\leq0\). Also
\[
A_{i,j-1}(d)+B_{i+1,j-1}(d)
=2d(i,j)-d(i,j-1)-d(i,j+1)\geq0.
\]
Each vertical sequence is discretely concave with zero endpoints, so
\(d(i,j)\geq0\). The recession cone is therefore zero.

Take a rational right inverse \(Y\) of \(N_S\), put \(y_0=-Y\mathbf1\),
and initially let \(a_s=1\) on \(S\) and
\(a_r=\max(1,1-n_ry_0)\) otherwise. Multiply \(y_0\) and all \(a_r\)
by a common positive denominator, leaving \(Y\) unchanged. They are now
integral, with selected margins zero and off-\(S\) margins at least one.
Choose an integer \(M_0\) and define
\[
B=2^m,\quad C_* =\max_r\sum_{s\in S}|(n_rY)_s|,\quad
M_0>\max\{4B+4,2(C_*+1)\},\quad \tau=M_0^{-1}.
\]
In the fixed zero-based physical row order put
\[
d_r=a_r+\tau^{r+1},\qquad y=y_0-Y(\tau^{s+1})_{s\in S}.
\]
Every \(d_r>0\); on \(S\), \(n_sy+d_s=0\), and off \(S\),
\(n_ry+d_r>1/2\). Thus \(x_*+\eta y\) has exactly \(S\) tight in
\(P_b(\eta)=\{x:\rho_r(b,x)+\eta d_r\geq0\}\).

Each primitive integral circuit of the interior normals has coefficients
of magnitude at most \(B\): use signed maximal minors and Hadamard's
inequality, since every normal has Euclidean norm at most two. For such a
circuit \(c\), \(c\cdot a\) is an integer. If nonzero, the perturbation
has magnitude at most \(B\tau/(1-\tau)<1/2\) and cannot cancel it.
If zero, the first nonzero power of \(\tau\) dominates all later terms by
the same geometric-series estimate. Hence \(c\cdot d\ne0\), including
circuits of parallel physical rows.

An explicit compatible amplitude is any rational
\[
0<\eta<\frac{1}{8mB^2A_*},\qquad A_* =\max_r a_r+1.
\]
The relaxed polytope is nonempty, full-dimensional and bounded. For an
independent \(m\)-row basis, an originally negative slack has magnitude
at least \(1/B\), since its denominator divides the basis determinant.
Cofactors have magnitude at most \(2^{m-1}\); a normal has at most four
nonzero entries. The change in any slack is at most
\(\eta(2mB+1)A_*<1/(2B)\). Thus no infeasible limiting basis becomes
feasible. A dependent active set would contain a circuit satisfying
\(c\cdot\ell(b)+\eta c\cdot d=0\). The first term is integral;
genericity handles zero, and \(\eta|c\cdot d|<1\) handles a nonzero value.
The relaxation is simple, and every vertex cone lies in the normal cone
of its original limiting vertex. Its entire fan is compatible.

For \(\xi=\sum_{s\in S}t_sn_s\), with every \(t_s>0\), the original
minimizers are exactly the face defined by \(S\): a positive sum of its
nonnegative slacks vanishes precisely there. The selected normal cell thus
has the positive saturated volume of that actual face as its weight, with
zero-dimensional volume one. Necessity is the tight-row rank argument of
Lemma~\ref{lem:support}, which also applies to zero-dimensional faces.
\end{proof}

This identifies the existential geometric support of the criterion.
It does not make every retained field inequality individually necessary for
positivity: different fields, compatible refinements and cancellations remain
available.

\subsection{A short calculus for every rank-six closure}\label{sec:closure-calculus}
There is a compact certificate for the closure computation itself. Its
zero-based physical row order agrees with the original atlas.

\begin{theorem}[Positive closure calculus]\label{thm:closure-calculus}
Among the 45 rank-six rhombi, take all disjoint unit-coefficient identities
with two rows on each side and with three rows on each side. There are 30
and 72, respectively, counting reversal as the same identity. Add
\begin{align*}
C_{2,1}+B_{1,2}+A_{1,3}+A_{2,0}+C_{4,2}+B_{4,0}
={}&A_{0,2}+C_{2,3}+B_{2,0}\\
&+C_{4,0}+A_{3,1}+B_{3,2}.
\end{align*}
These 103 positive identities generate exact physical-row closure for every
subset of the 45 rows. The original 102-identity calculus first fails at
generating-set cardinality six.
\end{theorem}

An identity gives two implications: if all rows on one positive side vanish,
every row on the other side vanishes. The 206 set implications produce 564
distinct single-conclusion Horn clauses. Iteration adds at most 45 rows.
The displayed identity is a direct equality of all 28 height coefficients;
the individual equality is not asserted new in the literature.

\begin{proof}[Proof from the small exact certificate]
Let \(R(S)\) be rule closure, \(C(S)\) actual cone closure, and \(V(S)\)
closure on the 166 supplied feasible integral heights. Sound positive
identities and feasible heights give
\[
R(S)\subseteq C(S)\subseteq V(S).
\]
For each target \(r\), introduce Boolean variables for membership in a set
\(M\). Include \(\neg x_r\), all Horn implications, and for every supplied
height where \(\rho_r>0\), the clause
\(\bigvee_{i:\rho_i>0}x_i\). A satisfying assignment is exactly a
rule-closed \(M\) with \(r\in V(M)\setminus M\).

The certificate supplies nine representative reverse-unit-propagation
refutations, with 6,488 additions, and 45 exact variable transports of their
complete input clause sets. For an added clause, negate every literal and
unit-propagate the existing database; a contradiction proves that clause
is a consequence. Every representative ends in a checked empty clause.
Exact clause-set equality under a variable bijection transports
unsatisfiability. Hence \(V(M)=M\) for every rule-closed set. With
\(M=R(S)\), monotonicity gives \(C(S)\subseteq C(M)=M\), proving
\(C(S)=R(S)\).

The independent checker reconstructs every literal rhombus and enumerates
every short identity. It checks 2,884 identity coordinates and
7,470 feasibility slacks, regenerates each input formula, checks every proof
addition and verifies every target transport. It uses no SAT solver or
extreme-ray completeness oracle. The 254,843-byte certificate takes about
five seconds to check with the independent Python standard-library program.

For minimality, the old-rule-closed set
\(S=\{4,17,18,23,31,41\}\) has actual closure
\[
\{2,3,4,7,8,9,11,17,18,23,24,26,31,35,39,40,41,44\}.
\]
Its interior rank rises from six to eight. The new identity and old rules
force these rows; the sum of the feasible heights tight on \(S\) has exactly
this zero set, proving equality. Separately, all seeds of size zero through
five are checked. Their populations are
\(1,45,990,14190,148995,1221759\), totaling 1,385,980; each has equal
old-rule and feasible-witness closures, which now equal actual closure.
This proves minimum cardinality six, without uniqueness or a minimum-size
presentation claim.
\end{proof}

The sandwich argument also shows that summing feasible heights tight on
any seed gives an integral relative-interior witness for its exact closure.
Together with Theorem~\ref{thm:realization}, this gives a constructive
physical-branch interface. The large rational positivity field and its
local-scalar obligations remain separate.
