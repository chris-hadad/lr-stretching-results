# Positive families, coefficient compensation and obstacles: companion outline

Draft outline connected to the [finite-box research note](finite-box.md).
This is a proposed organization of established results and open questions,
not a claim that a complete companion manuscript or every standalone
certificate package is finished.

## Central question

Why can local terms be negative while every known complete LR polynomial in
the examined classes is ordinary-positive? A useful account should connect
whole-count formulas, normal-cycle corrections and interior surplus. It must
also show where a sufficient criterion fails, since those failures distinguish
an incomplete representation from a possible whole-object counterexample.

## Dependency discipline for a full manuscript

Each theorem should give its ordinary LR triple or complete construction,
the real-polytope and integer-lattice correspondence, all parameter walls,
degree/codegree premises and an exact coefficient argument. Separate universal
symbolic steps from finite certificates. Give a local command for every
advertised replay and identify any retained external count observations.
The [structural dependency map](../results/structural-positivity/README.md)
is the current starting point; source-proof availability is a different
status from a completed portable atlas replay.

## Most informative next tests

The finite normal horizon offers a bounded route toward an all-rank ambient
coefficient theorem: complete the untouched connected classes at ranks thirteen
and fourteen, with the correct primitive lattices and every correction
incidence. If their minima are positive but below 1/3000, the uniform constant
must decrease. A negative local value defeats that sufficient constant or
criterion, not KTT by itself.

The strongest alternative is whole-rank-six positivity from uniform interior
or chamber inequalities. On the construction side, the unclosed rational
transport directions and larger matching flanks offer complete objects with
specific missing sign questions. A useful negative must survive the full LR
lift and an independently established degree and count model.

This publication session executes none of those new scientific tests.
Their purpose here is to make the reusable methods and unresolved questions
clear enough for another mathematician to assess or pursue.
